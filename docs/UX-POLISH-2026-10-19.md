# Desk UX polish — 2026-10-19

Artem requested alignment, organization and UX improvements, and reported a missing icon while the
window was open. This work is separate from the paused release-validation goal.

## Changes

- Shared amber bolt artwork for the tray, Desk title bar, taskbar and application header. The Windows
  MSVC executable embeds 16–256 px icons; the installed shortcut explicitly selects that icon. The
  running Desk sets the same app identity as its shortcut.
- A consistent light/dark palette, readable secondary text, 14 px body text, 22 px page headings,
  32 px controls, and shared margins and spacing.
- Brand/connection status and navigation have separate rows. Inbox cards have a clear selected state,
  wrapping titles, and accessible question/batch labels. Paging controls appear only when needed.
- Question details distinguish the answer area from source information. Immediate-send choices are
  explained, forms fit narrow panes, and text/form submission has a clear primary button.
- Agents and Owners use readable cards instead of wide tables. Settings has separate Notifications,
  Phone replies and Owners sections with independent scroll positions. Phone fields align and setup
  guidance is expandable.
- Consistent page headings, improved empty/offline states, Enter-to-search in directories and History,
  and a clear search-reset action for agent/owner directories.

## Inbox refinement

Artem found the Desk cluttered and said past chats looked like active questions requiring a reply.
Past questions now start collapsed, including when the Inbox is empty and when returning to the Inbox.
Their disclosure has a stable identity, so a changing result count does not unexpectedly expand it.
Persisted expansion from an earlier app run is reset on first display.

Active and past selections are separate. Opening or refreshing the Desk selects only a waiting
question; a past question needs an explicit click. Collapsing the past section clears its detail view.
When nothing is waiting, the detail pane says “You’re all caught up.” Past questions show their outcome
and “No reply needed”; priority, urgency and overdue-deadline cues are reserved for active questions.
Defaulted, withdrawn and not-needed outcomes retain their distinct meanings.

Active cards lead with the question title. Routine P2 badges are omitted, while P0/P1 remain visible.
Batch checkboxes appear only after “Select several”; cancelling selection leaves unsent drafts intact.
Restored batch selections remain available after an upgrade.

sizes are retained. The Desk release build completed successfully. In the isolated `ux-calm-1019`

## App-wide decluttering

Artem asked to carry the quieter Inbox approach through the rest of the app: keep things needing his
attention obvious and reveal secondary details and actions only when needed. The existing amber
accent, system-theme palette, proportional type and left-aligned layout are retained. This pass changes
information hierarchy rather than introducing another visual style.

- Navigation keeps Inbox, Agents, Board and Settings visible. History, Work holds and Activity live
  in a named More menu, which shows the current page when one of those pages is open.
- Questions keep their body, answer options and decision warnings visible. Supporting links, optional
  notes and delivery records are expandable; source and notification controls follow the answer.
  “Mark as not needed” moves into Other actions. A retained note is labelled as included even when its
  editor is collapsed. Unresolved delivery failures stay visible; a later successful attempt for the
  same destination clears the warning.
- Agents lead with their name and current work. Chat navigation, last-seen information and mute
  controls are under Details and actions. Proposed owner names retain a visible Review name action.
- Owners awaiting approval are first within the current result page, with their approval controls
  visible. Routine permissions and notification controls are expandable. Settings and Owners indicate
  reviews in the loaded owner page; this is not a new global pending-owner count.
- Phone replies begin with their status. Setup, credentials, diagnostic timestamps and refresh are
  expandable. Pending replies and errors remain visible. Setup still explains that question text is
  shared before saving. The optional count-only phone summary is collapsed, and Save notifications
  appears only when notification settings differ from the saved values.
- Activity starts with four measures and any waiting decisions, with an Inbox shortcut. Full period
  comparisons, breakdowns, methodology and export live under Detailed activity. Baseline and verdict
  editors are separate disclosures. Unknown history remains unknown, and defaults remain distinct
  from human answers.
- Board filters show their current scope, with filter choices in a menu. Handoffs near their claim
  timeout are labelled before expansion. Claim timeout settings are secondary details. Work-hold scan
  warnings remain visible while paths and timestamps are expandable; import and add-copy forms are
  disclosed only when requested. Exclusion editing stays visible in configuration.
- Paging controls appear only for multiple pages; redundant directory refresh controls are removed
  because those screens already refresh automatically. Search clearing remains available.
